Admissions Simulator

You think you know who gets in? Prove it.

Admissions Simulator is a browser game where you read a real, anonymized college applicant profile sourced from Reddit's r/chanceme and r/collegeresults, then predict which schools on their list admitted them. Submit your guesses and watch the real outcomes reveal one by one, compared against your read. Profiles show GPA, test scores, extracurriculars, awards, and demographics, the same file an admissions officer would review. No signup is required to play, and every profile is anonymized before it appears in the game.
